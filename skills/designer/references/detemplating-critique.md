# De-Templating Critique

Load during Step 2 while invoking **`frontend-design`**. Apply to the Step 1 base system.

## Generic Default Clusters (revise if matched without brief justification)

1. Warm cream (~`#F4F1EA`) + high-contrast serif display + terracotta accent
2. Near-black + single acid-green or vermilion accent
3. Broadsheet layout: hairline rules, zero radius, dense newspaper columns

If the user's brief **explicitly** requests one of these, keep it — otherwise change the conflicting axis.

## Questions (answer each; revise plan if answer is weak)

### Subject

- What is the **one concrete subject** and **audience**?
- What is this page's **single job**?
- Do palette, type, and layout pull from the subject's world (materials, vernacular, artifacts)?

### Hero / opening

- Is the hero a **thesis** about the subject — not a template (big stat + gradient + three cards)?
- If using a number, stat, or gradient — why is that the best form for **this** subject?

### Typography

- Would these display/body pairings appear on **any** random SaaS landing page?
- Is type treatment a **personality** choice, not neutral delivery?

### Structure

- Do numbered markers (01/02/03) encode real sequence — or decoration?
- Do dividers, eyebrows, and labels encode information — or filler?

### Signature & risk

- What is the **one memory point**?
- Where is **one aesthetic risk** taken and justified?
- Is boldness concentrated in the signature with quiet surroundings?

### Copy (if writing text)

- User-facing names (what people control), not system jargon?
- Active voice on actions; failures explain cause + fix; empty states invite action?

## Revision rule

Any "no" or "generic" answer → revise that dimension in § Revised Direction before Step 3.

Do not implement until revisions are recorded.
